Selected projects

Funded projects

RECOVER

CIS PI: Serkan Ayvaz · Period: 01/10/2025–30/09/2028

RECOVER develops digital twin methods for the southwestern Baltic Sea, with a focus on environmental monitoring and the generation of what-if scenarios. CIS contributes AI models, data architecture, and software infrastructure for integrating time-series data, hyperspectral imagery, and simulation-based modelling.

VANTAGE

CIS PI: Sadok Ben Yahia · Period: 01/01/2026–31/12/2028

VANTAGE develops AI-supported automation for vulnerability assessment and penetration testing. CIS contributes to multi-agent AI, chatbot infrastructure, and LLM-based components for the VANTAGE platform.

TESAR

CIS PI: Sadok Ben Yahia · Period: 01/09/2025–29/02/2028

TESAR develops training, simulations, and digital tools for evidence-informed policymaking. The project supports early-stage researchers with practical skills for translating research into policy advice.

Multimodal data processing of Earth observation data

CIS PI: Serkan Ayvaz · Period: 01/07/2024–31/12/2025

This project develops data pipelines and AI models for multimodal Earth observation and environmental data. CIS work includes models for shallow water inundation, SAR imagery processing, edge-computing architectures, image segmentation, and anomaly detection.

Aircraft Maintenance Software: collaboration project with ASA

Period: 01/02/2024–31/12/2025

The ASA collaboration transforms aircraft maintenance software through automated digitisation of maintenance documents, large-scale maintenance data handling, reporting and analysis, and data provenance support. The work supports search, validation, and management of aircraft maintenance status.

LAIA: Laboratory on Artificial Intelligence in Albania and Kosovo

Period: 01/12/2023–31/12/2026

LAIA develops and implements a master-level AI curriculum for higher education institutions in Albania and Kosovo. The project supports capacity building in artificial intelligence through collaboration across universities.
