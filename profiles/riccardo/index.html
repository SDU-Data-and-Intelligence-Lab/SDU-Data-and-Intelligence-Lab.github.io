About me

I am an Italian PhD researcher in artificial intelligence and information retrieval at the University of Southern Denmark.

My background combines humanities, engineering, and computer science, with studies at Università Politecnica delle Marche and Politecnico di Milano, international experience in Germany and Denmark, and professional experience as a Software and DevOps Engineer.

My current research explores large language models, retrieval-augmented generation, dataset discovery, metadata augmentation, and agentic information-retrieval systems. I also teach AI and supervise student projects.
