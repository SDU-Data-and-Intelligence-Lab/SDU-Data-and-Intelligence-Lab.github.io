About me

I am an Assistant Professor at the University of Southern Denmark. My research lies at the intersection of context-aware and explainable AI, physiological sensing, mobile health, and human-computer interaction.

I work with semi-supervised and self-supervised learning to reduce dependence on annotated health data. I received my PhD in Health Technology from the Technical University of Denmark, where I studied ambulatory monitoring and diagnosis of heart arrhythmias.

Research interests

  • Digital health
  • Explainable AI
  • Physiological sensing
  • Mobile health
  • Self-supervised learning
  • Human-computer interaction
