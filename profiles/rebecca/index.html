About me

I am a Research Assistant and Engineer at the SDU Center for Industrial Software in Sønderborg. I contribute to applied software activities in the Data & Intelligence Lab and teach web development.

My teaching covers the design and implementation of front-end and back-end web applications, including web communication, security, performance, and responsible software development.
