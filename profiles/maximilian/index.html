About me

I am an Assistant Professor at the University of Southern Denmark and Deputy Head of Programme for the Bachelor's and Master's programmes in Software Engineering at SDU Sønderborg.

My research focuses on foundational unsupervised learning and applied machine learning. A distinctive application area is natural-language processing for password forensics, including semantic methods based on word embeddings and similarity search.

I am also interested in the capabilities and potential limitations of agentic AI, and I enjoy teaching and supervising scientific work.

Research interests

  • Unsupervised learning
  • Applied machine learning
  • Natural-language processing
  • Password forensics
  • Agentic AI
  • Similarity search
