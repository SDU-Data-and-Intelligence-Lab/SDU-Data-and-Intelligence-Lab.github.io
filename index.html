Introducing the lab

The Data & Intelligence Lab studies big data, artificial intelligence, and intelligent systems with an emphasis on practical, testable work.

Centre for Industrial Software, Maersk Mc-Kinney Moeller Institute, SDU - Sonderborg

We keep the site deliberately spare: one clear landing page for the lab, and separate pages for publications and people. That makes the structure easier to scan and easier to update.

The work itself sits around data engineering, intelligent systems, proof-of-concept systems, and evaluation on real problems. The goal is useful results rather than visual noise.

Research directions

Four lines that shape the lab work.

Data engineering

Shaping datasets, pipelines, and evaluation-ready data for analysis and deployment.

Intelligent systems

Designing systems that use machine learning and AI in ways that are practical and measurable.

Proof of concepts

Turning ideas into working prototypes that can be tested with real data and constraints.

Experiments and evaluation

Measuring what changes, what holds up, and what fails when methods are tested on actual problems.
