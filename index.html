Data & Intelligence Lab

We work on big data, artificial intelligence, and intelligent systems, with an emphasis on what holds up in real use.

Centre for Industrial Software, Mærsk Mc-Kinney Møller Institute, SDU · Sønderborg

We study the methods, data, and systems around AI. The lab looks at both the technical pieces and the surrounding practice needed to make results trustworthy and useful.

This draft keeps the structure intentionally sparse. The goal is a clean home page that can later grow into separate pages for research, publications, people, and supervision.

Research

Four lines that shape the lab work.

Data engineering

Shaping datasets, pipelines, and evaluation-ready data for analysis and deployment.

Intelligent systems

Designing systems that use machine learning and AI in ways that are practical and measurable.

Proof of concepts

Turning ideas into working prototypes that can be tested with real data and constraints.

Experiments and evaluation

Measuring what changes, what holds up, and what fails when methods are tested on actual problems.
