Data & Intelligence Lab

We study big data, artificial intelligence, and intelligent systems. We value collaboration through funded research, industry partnerships, and student projects.

Research

Research directions

  • LLM-in-the-middle for Physical AI

    Semantic reasoning and decision support for safety-critical perception, planning, and control.

  • AI interpretability

    Interpretable machine learning and explainable models for data-intensive systems.

  • Multimodal AI and environmental monitoring

    Earth observation data, image segmentation, anomaly detection, and digital-twin style analysis.

  • NLP and semantic password forensics

    Word embeddings, similarity search, semantic password guessing, and applied language technologies.

  • Digital health and wearable sensing

    Mobile and wearable sensing for rehabilitation, activity tracking, stress recognition, and adaptive health software.
