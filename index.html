Data & Intelligence Lab

We study big data, artificial intelligence, and intelligent systems. We value collaboration through funded research, industry partnerships, and student projects.

Research

Research Directions

  • AI & Information Retrieval

    We study LLMs as retrieval agents for dataset discovery and evidence finding, combining metadata augmentation, hybrid search, GraphRAG-style provenance, and agentic search when resources are sparse or weakly described.

  • LLM-in-the-middle for Physical AI

    We explore how multimodal foundation models can sit between perception, planning, and control in safety-critical settings, with an emphasis on semantic reasoning, traceability, and runtime safety supervision.

  • AI interpretability

    We work on interpretable machine learning for systems that need to be understood, audited, and trusted, including feature attribution, diagnostics, and explanations that stay useful as data shifts over time.

  • Multimodal AI and
    environmental monitoring

    This area brings together Earth observation, sensor fusion, and digital-twin analysis. We study image segmentation, anomaly detection, and AI-supported monitoring of changing environments.

  • NLP and semantic
    password forensics

    We study NLP methods for password analysis and related security problems. Word embeddings, semantic retrieval, and retrieval-augmented methods reveal structure in user-generated secrets.

  • Digital health and
    wearable sensing

    We work with mobile and wearable data in rehabilitation and stress monitoring, with a focus on adaptive models that can learn from noisy real-world signals and patient-generated data.
